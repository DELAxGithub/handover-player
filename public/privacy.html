Privacy policy

What is stored

When you start a review we store the video link you pasted, a title taken from its file name, and the time of creation. When you comment we store the comment text, its timecode, and the name you typed (or "Anonymous"). We do not ask for an email address or password. Your browser also keeps a local history of your recent reviews and your chosen display name on your own device.

Where it is stored

Review data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database hosted in the Tokyo region. The site is served by Vercel. These providers process the data on our behalf.

Who can see it

Anyone who has the review link can view the review and its comments. There are no accounts or access controls beyond the link.

How long it is kept

Reviews and comments are kept until they are deleted. Links are created with a 7-day expiry date, but automatic expiry and deletion are not currently enforced. To have a review deleted, send us its link (see below).

Analytics and cookies

Handover does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Contact and deletion requests

Handover is operated by DELAX. To request deletion or ask a question, open an issue at github.com/DELAxGithub/handover-player/issues (do not post private information in the issue).

See also the terms of use.
