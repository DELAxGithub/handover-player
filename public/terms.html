Terms of use

What Handover is

Handover lets you paste a link to a video you already host (for example on Dropbox), play it in the browser, and leave comments pinned to timecodes. Handover does not upload or store your video; it only plays the link you give it.

Price

Handover is free to use. No account is needed.

Who can see a review

Anyone who has a review link can view it and add comments. Share links only with people you trust, and do not use Handover for material you could not safely share by link. Keep in mind that the video itself stays under the sharing settings of the service that hosts it.

Your responsibilities

Only paste links to videos you have the right to share and review. Do not post unlawful content in comments.
